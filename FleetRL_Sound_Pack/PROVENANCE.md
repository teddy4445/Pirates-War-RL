## Honest review status

File-level verification and browser decoding/playback tests ran. The WAV loop endpoints match exactly, and the files contain no digitally clipped samples. Actual listening through speakers/headphones was not performed. Final perceived loudness, comfort, stylistic fit and classroom mix approval remain open.

Ogg files are optional compressed derivatives. The canonical WAV files and their SHA-256 hashes define this release. Compressed output can differ across encoder versions; byte-identical reproducibility was verified for WAVs, not asserted for every possible Ogg build.
